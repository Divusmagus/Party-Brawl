PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.3
Released 2026-11-20

MUSIC AND SOUND
- Custom music system. Menu, stage and character songs can now be real song files, with the code-made
  chiptune as the fallback for anything that doesn't have one yet.
- Character themes: Frica ("Legend of Frica the Poor") and Madog ("A Knight's Oath"). A fighter's theme
  cuts in over the stage music when they're the first to break their opponent's bar in a round, or when
  they won the previous round. The most recent trigger wins; the winner's theme keeps playing on the
  victory screen. Songs are levelled to the same loudness and loop with a short crossfade.
- Stage songs: "Ballad of the Brogade" on Goldenfields and Spine of the World (it carries on without
  restarting if you go from one to the other), "D.I.C.K.S.: The Final Thrust" on Vogler, and "Starfire
  Extermination" on the Trafficker Ship, "The Booty Hunters' Plunder" on Erebus's Lair and "Neon Under
  Nuclear Skies" on Tahoe. Every stage now has its own song.
- Menu music: "Shadow Protocol" plays on the title screen, character select, settings and the online lobby.
- Victory jingle: plays once when the match is won, then the menu music comes back in. (A winner with a
  theme song gets their theme instead.)
- New Settings screen (title screen and pause menu) with Master, Music and Sound effects volume sliders,
  saved on your device. It replaces the old Music/Sound on/off buttons.

ONLINE
- Adaptive input delay: the host measures the ping before the match and sets the input delay for both
  players: 2 frames on a good connection (same as before), 3 at ~70-120 ms, 4 at ~120-180 ms, 5 above that.
  Slow connections (mobile data, US-UK) now rewind and replay much less, so fights feel smoother.
- The game can also rewind further on slow connections before it has to pause and wait.
- The ping readout at the bottom of the screen also shows the delay in use.

FIGHTERS
- Heavy Rail now has hand-drawn pixel art: the character, a crackling plasma bolt with a green burst on impact,
  jump jets when Power Armor Drop launches him, and a big fiery shockwave with flying rubble when he lands.
- Laser Sweep finally shows its laser: a thick red beam from the rifle across the whole area it hits.
  (Before, the move hit but nothing was drawn.)
- Owen Hale now has hand-drawn pixel art: the character, muzzle flashes and tracer rounds for the ACR burst,
  a tumbling smoke grenade that pops on landing, a thick pixel-art smoke cloud that billows out, churns and
  thins away, a red targeting flash on a CRIT, a whoosh on a MISS, and big red ship lasers for Captain's Orders.
- Tedd the Friendly now has hand-drawn pixel art, drawn as the biggest fighter in the game (his hitbox is
  unchanged). His heavy leaves a wide arc of fire, Pain Train kicks up a cloud of dust and speed lines,
  Board with a Nail lands with a big comic bonk star, and WANNA BE FRIENDS? has a proper bear hug: arms
  wide, the squeeze (with green squeeze lines and a little heart over his new friend), then the throw.
- Bof now has hand-drawn pixel art: the character, a big power fist impact with a steel shockwave, a shotgun
  blast of fire, smoke and pellets from the barrel, yellow claw rakes on every Gone Feral hit, and a green
  radiation glow while his ghoul healing kicks in.
- Big Chief now has hand-drawn pixel art: shirtless, pot-bellied and in a Santa hat, swinging a picket sign.
  Reindeer Stampede sends real galloping reindeer (the first with a glowing red nose) that rear up and vanish
  in a puff of snow when they hit. Spirit Totem rises out of the ground as a carved reindeer totem with a
  glowing ring, then sinks away. General Strike is a picket line of angry elves who shove when they connect.
  Union Break shows a steaming mug of cocoa.
- All six batch-2 fighters now have hand-drawn art. The old vector puppets stay as the fallback.

BALANCE
- Calder's passive is now Field Chemist (it overlapped too much with Bof's healing passive). Every 12-20s
  he crafts a random chem: Jet (25% faster, cooldowns recover 50% faster for 6s), Med-X (25% less damage
  taken for 6s), Addictol (clears poison, Pax, Berserk, MUDDLED, hexes and slows) or a Stimpak (heals 7%
  of max health). The old perk (healing starts after 2s instead of 3s) is gone. Cooldowns unchanged.
