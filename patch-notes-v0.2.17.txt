PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.17
Released 2026-11-24

Four new fighters! All four are in vector art for now; sprites, portraits and theme songs are on
the way. None of them have had a proper balance pass yet, so expect numbers to change after you
play them.

NEW FIGHTER: KUCK HOLD (Waterdeep: Dragon Heist)
- The Zhentarim boss with a fiend patron, half the gold, and the stuffed body of David, the gnome
  who robbed him. A tricky mid-range fighter with 1020 health.
- STUFFED DAVID (Special 1): hurls stuffed David at them (62, lobbed). David stands where he lands
  as a statue that blocks enemies (hop over him) and their shots. Walk over him to pick him up and
  reset the cooldown; otherwise he's gone when the 6s cooldown runs out.
- PUNT THE IMP (Special 2): drop-kicks his imp (badly disguised as David) along the ground. It runs
  straight ahead, hops down off ledges, and bursts into hellfire (64 Fire) on the first enemy it
  reaches. 3s cooldown.
- I'M WATCHING YOU (Super): Kuck fades from sight for 6s; only his glowing white eyes show, and he
  moves 30% faster. Every second a psychic pulse hurts everyone near him and gives them a stack of
  WATCHED. Each stack makes Kuck's hits on them do 5% more damage, up to 6 stacks (+30%); the
  stacks last 6s.
- VENGEANCE (passive): while David's face is out there watching (statue David on the field, or the
  imp in its David disguise running around), Kuck takes 20% less damage and 30% less knockback.
  The David in Da Fuck Den doesn't count: he's part of the stage.

NEW FIGHTER: VEGAGATH HARDHEART (Waterdeep: Dragon Heist)
- A big, simple-minded goliath fighter with a glaive. A Heavy with 1200 health.
- GLAIVE THROW (Special 1): hurls his glaive straight ahead (70). It spears the first enemy it hits:
  a long stun as they're pinned and pushed back, never a wall splat. 5s cooldown.
- SECOND WIND (Special 2): dashes forward and catches his breath on the way, healing 8% of his max
  health. 10s cooldown.
- THE HELICOPTER (Super): high on whatever Alaric gave him, he strips off and charges in,
  helicoptering like a saw blade: eight quick hits and a big finish that launches. Armored while
  he whirs. Censored, obviously.
- ACTION SURGE (passive): a light attack has a 30% chance, and a heavy a 15% chance, to instantly
  go again with no input.

NEW FIGHTER: SQUIRM (Princes of the Apocalypse)
- A hexblade who never draws his blade, just Eldritch Blast, and argues out loud with Daevic, a
  voice nobody else can hear. A zoner with 980 health.
- ELDRITCH BLAST (Special 1): two quick bolts of force in a straight line, each pushing them back a
  little. 1.5s cooldown.
- SPECTER (Special 2): a specter circles him for 5s. Anyone who hits him in melee takes Dark damage,
  and it gives him a small grey shield (6% of his max health). 7s cooldown.
- THUNDER STEP (Super): a thunderclap blasts everyone near him (180, unblockable, big knockback),
  and he teleports to the safest spot: the place on the ground or a fixed platform furthest from
  every enemy. Moving platforms (like the Da Fuck Den chandelier) don't count.
- TWO MINDS (passive): Daevic watches his back. Once every 10s, a hit from behind is blocked for him.

NEW FIGHTER: NETTOR HALLOS (Descent into Avernus)
- A Way of the Drunken Master monk and the party's usual Fat Peggy driver. A fast rushdown fighter
  with 900 health.
- FLURRY OF BLOWS (Special 1): a four-hit flurry, then he stumbles back out of reach. 3s cooldown.
- STUMBLE (Special 2): an erratic, lurching dash straight through people (untouchable for most of
  it); once he's through, he spins round and kicks. 4s cooldown.
- FAT PEGGY! (Super): he jumps in the party's infernal war machine and drives it across the whole
  stage, running over everyone on the ground (200, unblockable). Jump it.
- DEFLECT MISSILES (passive): a 30% chance to swat an incoming projectile back at whoever threw it,
  at 60% damage. Supers don't count.
