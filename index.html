Controls

Click a key or button to change it, then press the new one (Esc cancels, Backspace clears it). Changes save in this browser.

In Solo, either set of keys works for you. On a phone or tablet, on-screen buttons appear: slide on the left pad to move (slide down to drop through a platform), and use the right-hand buttons to jump, block and attack. Hit the other player into a wall hard enough and you get a Wall Splat for bonus damage. The super meter fills as you deal and take damage.
