PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.12
Released 2026-11-21

FREE FACING
- Fighters no longer turn to face the opponent automatically. You face the way you last pressed Left or Right,
  on the ground or in the air, and keep that facing when you let go.
- Pressing a direction and an attack on the same frame turns first, then attacks.
- You can't turn during an attack, while being hit or while stunned.
- Blocking only covers the side you're facing. Hits from behind get through. Hold Block and tap a direction
  to turn your shield around without moving.
- Jumping over someone now leaves you facing away from them until you turn: cross-ups are real.
- The CPU turns to face you whenever it attacks, blocks or uses a special, so it plays about the same as before.

SOREN
- Dragon Breath (Frost Breath in dragon form) is now a wide cone. Each puff is a fan of four breath clouds from
  his mouth: one angled up, one straight, and two angled down that reach the ground by the end of the range.
  It now hits anything in front of him, including short fighters standing right up against him (they used to
  slip under it). Each puff still hits only once, so damage and range are unchanged.

ART
- Soren: new silver dragon sprite sheet with more animation frames (hover, gallop, claw, wing sweep, breath,
  cast, roar) and an updated character sheet. His air heavy no longer draws the generic swoosh trail; the
  sprite has its own light burst.
