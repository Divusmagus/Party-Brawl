PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.16
Released 2026-11-24

NEW FIGHTER: DINO (Dragonlance)
- A stealthy sniper ranger who shoots from very far away and carries trick arrows. A light, quick
  zoner with 1050 health. Vector art for now.
- LONG SHOT (Special 1): tap for a quick arrow, or hold to draw the bow back (up to half a second)
  for a harder, faster arrow that crosses the whole stage. 0.75s cooldown.
- TRICK ARROW (Special 2): fires the next trick arrow in his quiver, always in this order:
  - FIRE: lobbed; leaves flames burning on the ground for 3s.
  - WHISTLE: a shrieking arrow that makes them flinch even through a block.
  - ROPE: shot up at an angle; where it sticks in a platform or wall, he zips over to it.
  An icon over his head, and in his Special 2 slot, shows which one is next.
- CALLED SHOT (Super): a red laser sight follows the enemy for about 1.5s, then locks and fires one
  huge arrow (240) that can't be blocked and flies through everything along the line. When it says
  LOCKED, jump or drop out of the line.
- EAGLE EYE (passive): his arrows hit harder the farther they fly, up to 25% more across the stage.
- Dino has his own theme song, "Hero's Fight".

ONLINE: WATCHERS AND TAKING TURNS
- An online room now holds up to 8 people. Anyone who joins once the player slots are full watches
  the fight live (same match, names and HUD, just no controls), even if they join mid-match.
- New room setting, ROTATION: who sits out after each match when people are watching. Off, Loser
  sits out (default), Winner sits out, or Winner + loser. The loser is whoever finished last (2v2:
  the losing team). People watching come in in the order they've been waiting, and take any CPU
  slot first.
- The victory screen shows a NEXT MATCH panel with who plays and who watches. The host can tap a
  player, then someone watching, to swap them (including themselves: the host can sit out and keep
  hosting while they watch).
- Rematch / Next match: players who stay keep their fighters, only newcomers pick.
  Character select: the rotation still happens, but everyone picks again.

NEW ART
- Every fighter now has new Autosprite art. All-new sprite sheets this time for Frica, Madog (plus
  a new portrait), Dask, Calder Grimm, Owen Hale and Heavy Rail.
- Madog's effects are new too: a ring of golden skull spirits for Spirit Guardians, a dust trail
  behind his charges, and the OH YEAH! brick wall now stands in front of him while he bellows, then
  bursts apart when he charges into it.
- Stand-ins for now: Frica's heavy reuses other frames; Dask Action uses his flinch frame; Owen and
  Heavy Rail hold a still stance when idle.

HITBOXES (fitted to the new art)
- Frica: light, heavy and Hex Blade hit at waist height (were chest height); Hex Blade reach
  64 -> 72. Air heavy covers head to feet.
- Madog: light reaches further, 70 -> 88, at chest height. Heavy covers above his head down to his
  waist.
- Dask: light at waist height. Heavy covers the whole overhead chop down to the floor. Air heavy in
  front of his waist. Reckless Rage covers head to feet.
- Calder Grimm: light covers chest to waist; heavy chest to floor; air heavy a little higher and
  further forward.
- Owen Hale: heavy moved down to waist height.
- Heavy Rail: light at waist height; heavy reaches lower (chest to waist).
- Reach is otherwise unchanged. Light, heavy and air heavy show their impact frames while the
  hitbox is live.

MUSIC
- Lives mode: when one fighter has more lives left than everyone else, their theme song plays,
  until they're out of lives or someone else takes the lead. A tie goes back to the stage music.
