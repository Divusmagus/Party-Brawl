PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.18
Released 2026-11-24

NEW ART
- KUCK HOLD: first real sprites, replacing his vector art: the hooded warlock in the long black
  gold-embroidered coat, glowing red eyes in the hood, dark green magic in his hands. A green flare
  from his palm (light), a big green burst from both palms (heavy), a green swipe and a huge
  forward blast in the air, pulling stuffed David from his coat and hurling him, a drop kick for
  Punt the Imp, and new walk, run, hit, block, stunned and knocked-down frames. New portrait.
  - Stuffed David no longer rides on his back; David only appears when Kuck throws him.
- KUCK'S IMP: now a tiny red devil in a David disguise (pointed red hat, fake white beard, green
  tunic, horns and tail showing). When it pops it swells up, bursts into a full hellfire explosion
  and smoke, and leaves its hat behind.
- DINO: first real sprites: the blond ranger in his ragged brown hooded coat with a wooden
  longbow. A bow jab, a big overhead bow swing, a kick and a downward bow swing in the air, nocking
  and drawing for Long Shot, an angled-up shot for Trick Arrow, dropping to one knee for Called
  Shot, and new walk, run, hit, block, stunned and knocked-down frames. New portrait.
- VEGAGATH HARDHEART: first real sprites: the grey-skinned goliath in a rusty breastplate with a
  long glaive. A lunging glaive thrust, an overhead chop into the ground, a flying thrust and a
  downward swing in the air, a javelin-style Glaive Throw, a charge and a deep breath for Second
  Wind, and new walk, run, hit, block, stunned and knocked-down frames. New portrait.
  - The Helicopter: he drops his armor and glaive and walks in, with a censor bar spinning like a
    fan blade. It's a plain black bar for now; custom censor bar art is coming.

MUSIC
- KUCK HOLD has his own theme song, "A Cuck's Gold".
- TENEBRUND STONESHAFT has his own theme song, "Tenebrund's Got an Appetite".
- Like the other fighter themes, these cut in when that fighter breaks the opponent's bar first or
  won the last round, play when they win, and play in Lives mode while they're in the lead.

BUG FIXES
- DINO: fixed getting stuck hanging in the air after a Rope arrow. If something blocks the zip
  (like the scaffold on Vogler), he now lets go of the rope after a moment and drops, and a zip
  can never last more than 1.5s.

BALANCE / HITBOXES
- DINO: the Whistle arrow now also slows them for 2s. The Fire arrow flies much flatter and faster
  (it used to sail over short fighters): it now hits short, medium and tall fighters from point
  blank to about 500 away.
- KUCK HOLD:
  - Stuffed David is much bigger as a statue (about the height of most fighters' chests, was knee
    high), so he actually blocks shots and is harder to hop over.
  - The David throw is much flatter and faster (was a high lob that only hit up close): it now hits
    from point blank to about 400 away.
  - I'm Watching You: he now also takes 30% less damage while it's active, so he can get in close.
- Hitboxes moved to match the new art for Dino, Kuck Hold and Vegagath Hardheart (reach unchanged).
  Their attacks now show their impact frames while the hitbox is live.
