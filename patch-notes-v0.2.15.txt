PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.15
Released 2026-11-23

NEW ART
- Bof: all-new sprites. A red-skinned ghoul with his Power Fist, a sawed-off shotgun on his back
  and a bandolier of shells. Rising Power Fist uppercut (heavy), lunging Power Fist punch, the
  shotgun draw-and-fire, a frenzied Gone Feral, and new hit, stunned, block and knocked-down frames.
  His effects (punch impact, shotgun blast, feral slashes, ghoul healing) are unchanged.
- Feng: all-new sprites with grown-up proportions. The half-orc war cleric in gold plate and a white
  tabard with his spiked gold mace. Mace thrust (light), overhead smash (heavy), a proper pamphlet
  flick for Pamphlet Toss, a glowing palm for Spiritual Weapon, mace-to-the-sky-then-point for Wicos
  Wills It, and new hit, stunned, block and knocked-down frames. His effects (pamphlets, spectral
  mace, pillar of light) are unchanged.

NEW MUSIC
- Feng has his own theme song, "Wicos Wills It".
- Salty Balls has his own theme song, "Squeezebox Pirate".
- Like the other fighter themes, these cut in when that fighter breaks the opponent's bar first or
  won the last round, and play when they win.

HITBOXES
- Bof: hitboxes moved to match the new art. Heavy now covers the rising uppercut, from his chest to
  above his head (was a chest-height box in front of him; reach 74 -> 60). Air heavy is now at chest
  height in front of him (was low, under his feet). His other attacks line up as they were.
- Feng: hitboxes moved to match the new art (same reach). Heavy: the overhead smash finishes low, so
  it now covers shoulder to knee (was chest height only). Air heavy now swings in front of his head
  and chest (was low, under his feet).

BEHIND THE SCENES
- Bof's and Feng's sprite sheets use the bigger cell size (like Tedd) so their weapons aren't clipped.
- The new theme songs are volume-matched to the rest of the soundtrack.
