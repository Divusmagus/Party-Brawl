PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.5
(Released 2026-11-20)

GOLDENFIELDS
- The hay bale in the wheat field is gone: the field is flat again.
- The wall and inn now hide everything behind them properly. Before, only the battlements were redrawn
  in front of fighters up on the wall walks, so big area effects (like Spirit Guardians) spilled over the
  wall face. Now the whole wall, inn and ground below the battlements sit in front of fighters on the
  walks and their area effects. Effects from fighters on the ground still show in front as normal.
- The lower wall walk now runs all the way to the right edge. The inn roof below it is no longer a
  separate platform, so there's no more stepping off the roof edge while "behind" the battlements.
- Balance check after the changes (792 CPU matches): every fighter between 39% and 58%.
  The stage screen's "Tough for" now says wildcards (they do worst here now); zoners are still favoured.

VOGLER
- Depth: the gate tower and the city wall now work like Goldenfields. Anyone up on the tower top or the
  wall walk is drawn behind the battlements and the wall/tower face, along with their area effects and any
  arrow fires burning up there. Fighters on the street are drawn in front as usual.
- Fire arrow volleys are a real threat now: each arrow has a 50% chance to stop on any rooftop or
  platform it meets and a 50% chance to punch straight through to whatever is below. The ground always
  stops them. Volleys now land all over, not just on the rooftops.
  (Volley damage per match roughly 2.4x what it was in CPU tests.)
- Landed arrows now lean the way they flew in: arrows from the right are no longer flipped once they land.
- The CPU now gets out from under a volley instead of trusting a rooftop to cover it.
- Balance check (792 CPU matches): every fighter between 42% and 64% (was 35-64%).
  The stage screen now says Vogler is tough for rushdown fighters; grapplers and zoners are still favoured.

TRAFFICKER SHIP
- The airlock rooms now have a solid roof. With the low gravity, lighter fighters could jump right out
  of the top of the stage.
- The space above each door is now a solid bulkhead all the way up to the roof. The only way in or out
  of an airlock room is through the doorway itself: you have to come down into the door to get through.
- A gentle push down in the space between the roof and the door tops, so you don't hang around up in the
  roof pocket (about 25% less hang time per jump). It shouldn't feel like a barrier.
- Fixed: a fast-moving fighter could get pushed out on the far side of a wall or door instead of being
  stopped. Walls now always push you back to the side you came from.
- Balance check (792 CPU matches): 33-58% (was 31-62%).

CONTROLS
- Dropping through a platform is now just Down: tap Down (S / Down arrow / stick or d-pad down) while
  standing on a platform. Holding Down still blocks, and Block + Jump still drops too.
- Block has its own input now: Q for Player 1, J or Num0 for Player 2, LB/LT on a controller
  (holding Down also blocks).
- Phones/tablets: slide down on the move pad to drop through a platform.
- Controls are rebindable: on the Controls screen, click any key or button and press the new one
  (Esc cancels, Backspace clears it, Reset to defaults puts everything back). Saved in your browser.
  Controller directions always come from the stick and d-pad; every button is rebindable, and you can
  add a jump button if you like.

HUD
- Frica's Life Drinker shield is no longer a plain grey block on the health bar: it's a silver ward
  styled to match each health bar theme, with a moving glint and a soft glowing edge.

PORTRAITS
- All 12 select-screen portraits replaced with a properly transparent version: no more white specks
  and halos around the edges (the old ones had their white background cut out by hand).
