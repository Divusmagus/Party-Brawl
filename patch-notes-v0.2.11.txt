PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.11
Released 2026-11-21

SUPERS
- Tenebrund's Belly Flop and Heavy Rail's Power Armor Drop no longer land on whatever platform happens to be
  under them. On the way down they fall through platforms, unless the opponent is standing on a platform right
  below and within range (190 for Belly Flop, 180 for Power Armor Drop); then they land on that platform and
  slam them there. Otherwise they come down on the ground, so the hit looks and plays like a real slam,
  mostly on platform-heavy stages like Vogler and Tahoe. Solid platforms still stop them as before.
