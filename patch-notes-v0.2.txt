PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2
Released 2026-11-20

STAGES
- Town Square (the old flat test stage) is gone. Six stages remain, one per campaign. A proper flat, hazard-free stage may come back later as its own thing.
- Vogler: new painted background (the burning town, the gate tower, siege engines on the hill).
  Platforms refitted to the art: house roof, market stall, gate tower top, wall walk.
  The gate tower's side is now a solid wall between the tower top and the wall walk.
- Tahoe: new painted background (ruins, the shack, the water tower, the pier and the boat at sunset).
  Platforms refitted to the art: ruin roof, shack roof, water tower catwalk, pier, boat deck.
  The old boat-cabin platform is gone (the painted wheelhouse sits too low to stand on).
  The lake now starts at the shoreline in the painting. Wading shows water round the legs instead of a flat blue box.
- Vogler: the flaming arrow volley is now hand-drawn: burning arrows in flight, a burst of sparks when one hits,
  and arrows that stick in the ground and burn (fire catches, flickers, then dies into smoke).
- Tahoe: the bloatfly is now hand-drawn: it buzzes in, dives to bite, flies off, and bursts in a yellow splat when you hit it.

FIGHTERS
- Calder Grimm now has hand-drawn pixel art: the character, his rifle round and muzzle flash, syringe darts
  (bigger, dripping ones for Overdose), and a glass-shatter burst when a dart hits.
- Calder's chems now show a little icon over whoever they hit while the chem lasts: a dripping scorpion tail
  (Scorpion Venom), a sleeping moon (Tranquilizer), a peace dove (Pax), a scared face (Yellow Belly), an anger burst (Berserk).

FIXES
- Online: you can now type the room code on phones and keyboards. Letters that are also fight controls
  (like R, G and H) were being swallowed by the demo fight running behind the menu.

BALANCE NOTES
- Vogler still favours Tenebrund a little (about 65%), Tahoe still favours Tedd a little (about 61%). Everyone else is 42-56%.

OTHER
- The title screen shows the version number. For online play, both players need the same version.
