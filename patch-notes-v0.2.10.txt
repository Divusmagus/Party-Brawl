PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.10
Released 2026-11-21

BALANCE
- Calder Grimm, Overdose (super): toned down after playtesting.
  - A repeat chem no longer stacks to double strength. It just resets that chem's timer.
  - Removed the double damage to shields. That was meant to go when the darts were made to
    get through blocks, and was left in by mistake. The chems still get through a block.
  - The six-dart cycle (all five chems in a random order, then one random) is unchanged.
- Calder Grimm, Hunting Rifle: damage 105 -> 95.
- Calder Grimm, Field Chemist (passive): new chem, Psycho. For 6s he deals 25% more damage.
  It joins Jet, Med-X, Addictol and Stimpak, so each craft is now a 1-in-5 pick.
- CPU testing: Calder's average win rate across all stages goes from about 57% to about 50%.

DASK REWORK
- Dask Action is now part of his passive (Bear Totem). When a big hit is about to land, he has a chance to bolt
  backwards out of the way, untouchable, and the hit misses. The harder the hit, the likelier: about 10% at
  90 damage, 25% at 120, up to 35% at 140+. 20 second cooldown.
- New Special 2, Barbarian Charge: he sprints straight at the opponent (about 200 units) and ends in a shoulder
  barge (25 damage, light knockback). His gap closer. 6 second cooldown.
- Bear Totem: 15% less Physical damage -> 5% less damage from every type except Psychic.
- Reckless Rage: hits 34/34/56 -> 30/30/50 (base, before his power), and a shorter lunge (about 125 -> 95 units).
- Power: 1.12 -> 1.06. The new tools made him much stronger in testing (up to ~67-82% before this),
  so his damage comes down a little to pay for them.
- CPU testing: about 54% before, about 51% after.

TEDD REWORK
- Pain Train finally drags people like it was always meant to. Before, Tedd and his victim shoved each other
  apart every frame, so he just ran into them like a wall. Now he carries them along (about 290 units across
  open ground) and they take damage the whole way.
  - A full drag in the open does about 60 damage in total.
  - If he pins them against the edge of the stage, he stops, the damage per tick doubles from then on, and
    the final slam adds the +30 wall bonus (WALL SPLAT!). A long drag into a wall does about 125.
  - Cooldown: 3.3s -> 7s. The working drag is much stronger than the old one.
- WANNA BE FRIENDS? (super): he now lunges forward about 110 units while reaching, and the grab reaches further
  (85 + the target's half-width, was 60) and catches short hops. Much harder to step away from.
  - Hug damage roughly cut in half to pay for it: squeezes 36 -> 22, throw 90 -> 55. About 206 for the whole
    hug with his power (was about 337).
- Board with a Nail: cooldown 1.7s -> 2.5s (damage unchanged).
- Mutant Hide (passive): 35% -> 15% less Poison and Explosive damage.
- CPU testing: about 60% before, about 56% after. (Fixing the drag alone pushed him to 78%.)

TENEBRUND
- Shrug It Off: a shrugged hit still does no damage and doesn't stop him, but its other effects now apply:
  Pain Train's latch and drag, Syringer chems, Guiding Bolt's mark and so on. He's meant to be bulky, not
  immune. (Before, shrugging Pain Train's 6-damage latch cancelled the whole drag.)
- Cheese Grater: the dash when nobody is in reach goes about 128 units (was 84). The grab reaches a little
  further (46 + their half-width, was 42), so it whiffs less. To pay for it, the cooldown is 2s both after
  a whiff and after a grab (was 1.5s / 1s).
- CPU testing: Tenebrund about 57% -> about 61% overall.

FENG REWORK
- New passive, Divine Decree (replaces Healing Word): Pamphlet Toss hits and Spiritual Weapon strikes each mark
  the target with a stack of Divine Decree, up to 5. His next light or heavy attack that lands (ground or air)
  spends them all for bonus damage: 1% of the target's max health per stack (about 50 at 5 stacks on a
  1000-health fighter). Without a new stack, they fade one at a time every 5s. The target shows a DECREE xN tag.
- Pamphlet Toss now heals him for 25% of its damage. Each quick repeat heals less (25% -> 15% -> 9% -> 5%
  minimum), and it resets after 4s without a pamphlet hit.
- CPU testing: about 46% before, about 52% after.
