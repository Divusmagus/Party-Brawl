PARTY BRAWL - PATCH NOTES v0.2.13
Released 2026-11-22

NEW ART
- Soren: all-new sprites with grown-up proportions (no more chibi), including every attack, his
  Silver Dragon roar, and a new hit, stunned and block set.
- Soren's Silver Dragon: all-new dragon sprites. Hover, gallop, claw, wing sweep, breath, roar, hit
  and block. The wing sweep is his heavy.
- Big Chief: all-new sprites, a little shorter than before (he's an elf), and a new character-select
  portrait.
- Big Chief's Union Break has its own animation: he plants his sign, sits down and has a coffee.
- Tenebrund: all-new sprites based on his campaign art: a big, bearded battlerager dwarf in spiked
  armour over a patched tunic. Shoulder-charge heavy, spinning-ball air heavy, a proper bear-hug-and-grind
  Cheese Grater, an armpit-sniffing Stink Cloud and a full Belly Flop.
- Tenebrund's effects are new too: a bubbling stink cloud, metal sparks on the Cheese Grater, a ground
  slam with flying rocks and dust on the Belly Flop, and his flies. The Stink Cloud is a see-through
  dome that fighters stand inside, with small puffs drifting up out of it. The Belly Flop crater sits
  on the ground behind the fighters.
- Tedd: all-new sprites: a grey-skinned super mutant with a flaming Shishkebab and his Board with a Nail
  strapped to his back. Overhead flaming chop, flaming air spin, a head-down Pain Train charge, the BONK,
  and the WANNA BE FRIENDS? bear hug and toss. He lumbers around at a fast walk instead of running.
  New grey character-select portrait too.

SOREN - SILVER DRAGON
- Hitbox matches the dragon: a body box (haunches to chest, ground to shoulders) plus a neck-and-head box.
  The tail and wing tips still can't be hit. In the air both boxes sit a little higher, like the hover art.
- He takes up real space: fighters get pushed out to the front of his chest (or the back of his haunches)
  instead of standing inside him.
- His normals are dragon-sized: claw, wing sweep, air claw and air tail reach about as far as the art.
- Unfazed: hits that deal under 30 damage (after his damage reduction) never make him flinch, and bigger
  hits have a 25% chance not to. He still takes all the damage. Unblockable attacks always make him flinch.
- Jumps 15% higher and glides 35% faster through the air.
- To pay for the above: damage taken -30% -> -20%, damage dealt +32% -> +20%.
- The CPU now judges distance from the edge of a fighter's body, so it attacks the dragon from the right range.

SOREN
- Frost Breath puffs that hit don't vanish any more: they stop hurting and fade out on the target, so you
  can see the breath landing up close.
- No white swoosh on his air heavy (the new sprite has its own light burst).

BIG CHIEF
- His animations now line up with his hitboxes: the punch, the sign chop, the air kick and the air
  sign swing are on screen while they can hit.
- Heavy hitbox reshaped to the overhead sign chop: from just above his head down to his hip (taller,
  same reach).

TENEBRUND
- His animations line up with his hitboxes: the punch, shoulder charge, air knee, spin and the
  Cheese Grater lunge are on screen while they can hit. (No hitbox changes.)

TEDD
- Bigger: about 9% taller and wider, body and hitboxes alike (body 60x106 -> 65x115). His attacks reach
  about 9% further and he is a slightly bigger target.
- His animations line up with his hitboxes, including Board with a Nail (the BONK swing is on screen
  while it can hit).
- Heavy hitbox reshaped to the overhead Shishkebab chop: from head height down to his knee.

SALTY
- Mush It is now three small sound waves in a tight cone instead of one huge wave. Each does 24 with a
  small shove. If all three hit, it's 72 (was 66) with the same big shove as before. They start stacked
  close enough that all three hit a standing fighter up close, and spread apart with distance, so a jump
  or a short fighter far away can slip some of them.
- His animations now line up with his hitboxes: the light punch, the accordion slam (heavy) and the air
  heavy swing are on screen while they can hit, not a few frames after.
- Heavy hitbox reshaped to the overhead slam (taller, same reach).
- Air heavy is a forward accordion swing at chest height now, not a stomp under his feet.

CALDER GRIMM
- Syringer cooldown 1.33s -> 2s.
- Tranquilizer stun 0.83s -> 0.75s (Syringer and Overdose).
- Field Chemist crafts more often: every 10-17s (was 12-20s).

HEAVY RAIL
- Health 1180 -> 1280.
- Power 1.15 -> 1.25 (everything he does hits about 9% harder).
- Power Armor Drop steers after the enemy while he's in the air and comes down fast after the top of
  the jump, so it's much harder to just walk away from. (Since v0.2.11 he falls through platforms on
  the way down, which made the drop slow and easy to dodge.)

STAGES
- NEW STAGE: Avernus (Descent into Avernus). A hellish wasteland where Baldur's Gate hangs on chains in
  the sky and Fat Peggy, the party's war machine, sits parked behind the fight. Flat cracked hellrock
  with two boulders to hop or stand on, and three cracked hilltops to climb (the centre crag is the
  highest). Music: "Fat Peggy".
- Avernus hazard: infernal war machines. About every 14 seconds a warning flashes on one side, then a
  war machine drives straight across the ground, kicking up dust: a devil on an Infernal Rider war bike
  (60 damage, easy to hop), the saw-wheeled Crusher (90, time your jump) or Fat Peggy herself (120,
  double jump or get up a hill). Unblockable, and it launches you the way it was going. The hilltops
  are safe.
- Da Fuck Den: the floor is a little lower, so fighters stand on the floorboards instead of floating at
  the back wall. Gravity there is a touch lighter (x0.9), so the balconies and chandelier take the same
  jumps as before.
- Stage select fits five stages to a row.
